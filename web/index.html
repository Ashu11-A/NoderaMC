NoderaMC

Minecraft without a server.

The world is split into 8×8-chunk regions, each simulated and validated by a small committee of player-run peers. Any player shares a world straight from the pause menu, and peers find each other through an untrusted tracker and rendezvous relay.

No central server is required — including this one. This domain runs a tracker and a relay as a convenience; both are replaceable, and neither holds authority over anybody's world.
